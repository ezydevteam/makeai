**Appearance → Addons → AI Knowledge Base → Articles** and **Categories** are where you write your help content — entirely by hand, since this addon doesn't offer AI-generated articles.

## Writing an article

An article has a **Title** (auto-generates a URL slug you can still edit), a rich-text **Body**, a short **Excerpt** shown in article listings, an optional **Category**, a **Sort Order**, and its own **Meta Title**/**Meta Description** for SEO. There's no tags field and no featured-image field — categories and the excerpt are what organize and preview your content. Save as **Draft** while you're still writing, or **Publish** when it's ready; there's no separate review/approval step, so publishing is immediate for anyone with access to this screen.

## Organizing with categories

Categories are flat — there's no nesting or parent/child hierarchy. Each has a **Name** (auto-slugged), an **Icon**, a **Sort Order**, a **Description** shown in the KB directory, its own SEO fields, and an **Active** toggle. A category with articles still assigned to it can't be deleted until those articles are reassigned or removed.

## Reading your KB analytics

**Analytics** shows Searches Today, Searches in 7 Days, an Answer Rate percentage, and Published Articles — each compared to the prior period. Below that: an **Unanswered Queries** list (search terms that returned no AI answer — your best source of "what content is missing"), **Top Search Queries**, **Top Articles** by views with their helpful-vote percentage, and an **Embed Status** breakdown showing how many articles are still pending AI processing.

## Why an article isn't showing up in search or AI answers

- It's still in **Draft** — only Published articles are searchable or used to ground AI answers.
- Its embed status is **Pending**, **Processing**, or **Failed** on the Analytics screen — a newly published article needs to finish processing before AI answers can cite it.
- It's assigned to an **Active: off** category, which can hide it from the category browse view even if the article itself is published.
