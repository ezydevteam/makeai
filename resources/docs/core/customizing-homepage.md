**Admin → Appearance → Themes → Settings → Home** builds your homepage from independent, drag-and-drop sections — no coding required.

## How the section builder works

Your homepage is a list of sections you can turn on or off individually: Features, Tools Showcase, How It Works, Pricing (only shown once billing is available on your license), Testimonials, FAQ, Social Proof, a CTA Banner, Latest Blog Posts, Newsletter, Custom HTML, Rich Text, an Image Carousel, and up to three Ad Slots. Click a section to expand it and edit its content, background, spacing, and visibility.

## Editing your main banner

The hero banner sits above the section list and always shows first — it isn't one of the draggable sections. Choose from six styles (Centered, Tools Grid, Split, Showcase, Enterprise, Featured), then set the Headline (supports a typewriter effect or a line break), Subheadline, Badge Text, and your main call-to-action button. The Tools Grid style also lets you hand-pick up to four featured tools shown right in the banner.

## Reordering and swapping sections

Drag any section by its handle to move it up or down — the list order is the order visitors see live.

## Letting an addon take over your homepage

At the top of the **Home** tab, a **Homepage** dropdown lets you hand your entire site root over to an installed addon instead of using the section builder — useful if you want your homepage to *be* the addon's own experience rather than a page that links to it. This dropdown only appears once you have at least one eligible addon active; on a stock install with none active, it's hidden and "Custom (theme default)" is your only option.

Two addons currently support this:

- **AI Chatbot** — visitors land directly on the full-screen chat interface (the same page as `/chat`) instead of a regular homepage.
- **AI Image Pro** — visitors land directly on the AI Image Pro generation/landing page.

Picking one replaces the entire rest of the Home tab with a short summary of what that addon will show, plus a link to manage that addon's own settings — the hero banner and section builder are hidden while an addon is selected, not just visually skipped. Choosing **Custom (theme default)** switches back and restores your hero and sections exactly as you left them.

If you later deactivate the addon you'd selected, your homepage automatically and silently falls back to your theme's normal homepage — it won't show an error to visitors.

## Why a homepage section isn't showing

- Its own visibility toggle is off, separate from whether it's in the list at all.
- The **Pricing** section only appears once billing is actually available on your license — see [Subscriptions and Plans](subscriptions-and-plans).
- An addon is selected in the **Homepage** dropdown, which hides the whole section builder (and hero banner) underneath it — switch it back to "Custom (theme default)" to get them back.
