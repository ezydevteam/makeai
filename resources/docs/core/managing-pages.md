**Admin → Content → Pages** builds standalone pages for your public site — About, legal pages, landing pages, anything that isn't a blog post. Pages support a parent/child hierarchy, and a few system pages (used elsewhere in the product) are protected from deletion.

## Creating and editing a page

Set a **Page Title** (a slug is generated automatically, editable if needed) and write the body in the built-in rich-text editor — including AI Assist buttons that can generate a title, content, excerpt, or SEO fields for you. Add an **Excerpt**, a **Featured Image**, and optionally a **Parent Page** to nest it under another. SEO fields (Meta Title, Meta Description, Meta Keywords, Open Graph Image) are on the same screen.

## Publishing, scheduling, and layout

The Publish button is a dropdown: **Save as Draft**, **Schedule Page** (pick a Publish At date/time), or **Publish Now**. Layout Options let you toggle the page title, breadcrumbs, featured image, and sidebar (with a left/right position) on or off individually, and set a **Container Width** (Default, Full Width, Boxed, or Stretched). A page can also be **password-protected** if you want to share it privately before it's public.

## Why a page isn't showing on the live site

- Its status is **Draft** or **Scheduled** for a future date rather than **Published**.
- It's password-protected and the visitor hasn't entered the password.
- It was moved to **Trash** — check the Trashed filter on the Pages list to restore it.
