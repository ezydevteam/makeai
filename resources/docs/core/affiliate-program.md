**Admin → Marketing → Affiliate** lets customers earn a commission for referring new signups and purchases — an Extended-license feature that also requires being switched on under **Settings → Features**.

## How someone becomes an affiliate

There's no separate application or approval step: anyone whose referral link brings in a signup automatically shows up in your **Affiliates** list, with their referral count, commission count, and total earnings. From that list you can **Suspend** or **Reinstate** an affiliate, but there's nothing to "approve" first.

## How commissions are calculated and approved

Set a commission type (percentage or fixed amount) and value under **Settings**, along with what counts toward it — first purchase only, all purchases, or subscriptions. Commissions are approved manually by default from the **Commissions** screen (individually or in bulk), or automatically if you turn on **Auto approve commissions**.

## Paying out affiliates

Payouts are admin-reviewed, not automated through a payment gateway: an affiliate requests a payout, and you review it on the **Payouts** screen, setting its status to Processing, Paid, or Rejected with an optional note. Enabled payout methods (PayPal, bank transfer, or store credits) are configured in Settings — PayPal and bank transfer just capture the affiliate's destination details for you to pay manually outside the system; "credits" pays out directly as store wallet credit.

## Why an affiliate isn't earning commissions

- The affiliate program isn't enabled under **Settings → Features**, or your license isn't Extended.
- The referral's cookie window (set in Settings, in days) expired before the purchase happened.
- The commission is sitting **Pending** and auto-approve is off — it needs manual approval on the Commissions screen before it counts toward payout.
- The affiliate's account has been **Suspended**.
