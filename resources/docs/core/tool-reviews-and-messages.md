**Admin → Communications → Tool Reviews** and **Messages** handle two different customer touchpoints: star ratings customers leave on individual AI tools, and messages submitted through your contact form.

## Moderating a tool review

Each review is a 1–5 star rating and comment a customer left on a specific AI tool's page. Click a review's status pill to **approve** or **disapprove** it — approving is what makes it visible on that tool's public page. You can also **Reply** to a review (with an option to auto-approve it at the same time you reply), or **Delete** it permanently. Filter the list by approval status or star rating to find what needs attention.

## Handling a contact message

**Messages** works like an inbox: unread submissions are marked as such until you open them, and each shows the sender's name, email, subject, and message. Reply directly from the detail pane — your reply is sent by email. Delete removes a message permanently; there's no separate spam-marking action, so delete is also how you clear unwanted submissions.

## Configuring your contact form

From the Messages screen's Settings, choose whether the Subject field is free text or a dropdown (and set the dropdown options), set the success message shown after submitting, set the notification email your team receives, and optionally turn on an **auto-reply** with its own subject and message (supports `{name}`, `{email}`, and `{subject}` placeholders).

## Why you're not getting notified of new messages or reviews

- The contact form's **Notification email** is unset or points to an inbox no one checks.
- A review needing a reply is sitting under a filter (like Status: Approved) that hides it — clear filters to see everything.
