**Admin → Content → Testimonials** and **FAQs** manage the trust-building content shown on your homepage and public pages — customer quotes and answers to common questions, entirely separate from the AI Assistant's own documentation search.

## Adding a testimonial

A testimonial needs a **Name** and the **Review** text itself; everything else is optional — Role, Company, Photo, a 1–5 star **Rating**, and a **Source** label (Manual, Google, Trustpilot, or AI). The **Active** toggle controls whether it shows publicly at all; **Featured** gives it priority placement in hero and spotlight sections. An **AI Generate** flow can draft a batch of sample testimonials from a company type, tone, and prompt if you need placeholder content to start with — replace these with real customer quotes before launch.

## Adding an FAQ

An FAQ needs a **Question** and an **Answer** (rich text), and can optionally be assigned to a **Category** — anything left uncategorized shows under "Uncategorized." The **Active** toggle controls visibility; deleting a category doesn't delete its FAQs, it just uncategorizes them.

## Why a testimonial or FAQ isn't showing

- Its **Active** toggle is off.
- For testimonials, the section you're looking at only shows **Featured** ones, and this one isn't marked as such.
- For FAQs, check you're looking in the right **Category** — an FAQ only appears under the category it's actually assigned to.
