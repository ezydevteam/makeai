**Admin → Marketing** also covers three broadcast tools: **Newsletter**, **Advertisements**, and **Announcements**.

## Sending a newsletter campaign

**Newsletter → Subscribers** lists everyone who's opted in, with their status and signup date. **Campaigns** are simple: a Subject, an Audience (newsletter subscribers, all opted-in users, active users, inactive users, pro users, or free users), and rich-text Content — there's no scheduling field, a campaign sits as a Draft until you click **Send**, which queues it immediately. A **Send test** action and a **Retry failed** action are also available. **Settings** controls the sending driver (internal only, Mailchimp only, or both), a double opt-in toggle, and a full popup builder for capturing new subscribers (trigger type, delay, title, styling).

## Placing ads

**Advertisements** works with named **ad zones** — fixed placement slots across your site like header banner, sidebar, or between AI tools. Each ad has a type (Image link, Custom HTML, or AdSense), a target zone, an audience (all visitors, guests only, logged in, free users, paid users), and an optional start/end schedule. This covers both your own self-serve ads (upload an image + link, or paste custom HTML) and a first-class Google AdSense integration, configured with your Publisher ID globally.

## Setting up an announcement

**Announcements** creates a scheduled, targeted message — Top Bar, Popup Modal, or In-App Notification — with a title, content, colors, an optional call-to-action button, a target audience, and how often it shows (always, once per session, or once per user), plus an optional start/end schedule.

## Why a campaign, ad, or announcement isn't reaching anyone

- A newsletter campaign stuck at **Draft** was never actually sent — Send is a separate, explicit action from saving.
- An ad's schedule window hasn't started yet, or has already ended.
- An announcement's **Active** toggle is off, or its target audience excludes the visitor you're testing with.
