**Admin → Content → Blog** covers writing posts, organizing them with categories and tags, moderating comments, and the blog's site-wide behavior settings.

## Writing and publishing a post

A post has a **Title** (auto-generates a **Slug**), a rich-text **Content** editor with AI Assist (generate title, content, excerpt, SEO, or tags), an **Excerpt**, a **Featured Image**, **Categories** (multi-select) and **Tags** (free-text), plus an **Author** and full SEO fields. The Publish button offers **Save as Draft**, **Schedule** (pick a date/time), **Private**, or **Publish Now**. Toggles let you mark a post **Featured** or **Pinned**, show a table of contents, exclude it from search engines, or disable comments/related posts for that one post specifically. The editor autosaves your draft roughly once a minute.

## Organizing with categories and tags

**Categories** have a name, description, optional parent category, icon, color, sort order, and an active/inactive toggle. **Tags** are simpler — name, description, and their own SEO fields — with a "Delete Unused" bulk action for any tag with zero posts attached.

## Moderating comments

**Admin → Content → Blog → Comments** lists every comment with status **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Spam**. Approve or mark as spam individually or in bulk from the list, or delete a comment permanently. Reader-submitted reports (with reasons) show on a comment's detail view if it's been flagged.

## The blog-wide settings

**Blog → Settings** covers five areas: general defaults (default author, reading-time calculation, RSS post count), archive/category display (posts per page, sidebar position), post-page display (layout width, related-posts algorithm, which metadata to show), social share buttons, and comment behavior — including a global **Enable comments** switch, **Require approval for all** comments, **Auto-approve logged-in users**, and whether guests can comment at all.

## Why comments aren't appearing

- **Enable comments globally** is off in Blog Settings, or the specific post has its own "Disable comments" override switched on.
- **Require approval for all** is on and the comment is still sitting in **Pending** — it won't show publicly until approved.
- The commenter posted as a guest while **Allow guest comments** is turned off.
