**Admin → Settings → GDPR** controls the cookie consent banner shown to visitors, without touching any code.

## Turning the banner on and choosing who sees it

**Enable Global GDPR Cookie Consent** switches the banner on site-wide. **EU/EEA Countries Only** limits it to visitors detected in those regions instead of showing it to everyone. **Banner Position** picks where it appears: Bottom Banner, Top Banner, or Center Modal.

## Writing the banner text and buttons

Under **Banner Content**, set the Title and Description text, plus the label on each of the four buttons: Accept All, Customize, Necessary Only, and Save Preferences.

## Matching your brand's colors

**Banner Colors** sets the background color, text color, button color, and button text color — pick colors that match your theme so the banner doesn't look bolted on.

## Linking your policies

**Policy Links** has a toggle to show Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy links directly on the banner, plus the two URL fields those links point to.

## Why the banner isn't showing

- **Enable Global GDPR Cookie Consent** is off.
- **EU/EEA Countries Only** is on, and you're testing from outside that region.
- The visitor already gave consent on a previous visit — the banner won't reappear until their stored preference expires or is cleared.
